# OpenQuest licensing

This document maps the different kinds of material in OpenQuest. It is not a
claim that OpenQuest can relicense material owned by someone else.

## Software

OpenQuest application source, Worker code, migrations, tests, WebMCP
implementation, and technical documentation are licensed under Apache-2.0
unless a file explicitly states otherwise. See [LICENSE](./LICENSE).
Future standalone OpenQuest protocol schemas and SDKs are also intended to use
Apache-2.0.

## Public factual data

Canonical factual records and metadata published by the official OpenQuest
network are intended for CC0-1.0 dedication where OpenQuest or the submitting
party has the necessary rights. This may include Quest and Challenge
identifiers and metadata, source or citation metadata, verification state,
timestamps, relationship or graph metadata, and structured factual datasets
produced by OpenQuest. See [LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt](./LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt).

## Authored expression

Original copyrightable expression submitted through a Quest, Challenge,
Contribution, or Review is licensed to the public under CC BY 4.0, to the
extent copyright or similar rights exist and the submitter owns or controls
the rights needed to grant that license. See
[CONTRIBUTION_TERMS.md](./CONTRIBUTION_TERMS.md) and
[LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt](./LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt).

## Evidence

Evidence links are references only. Linked papers, datasets, websites, and
other third-party works keep their original copyright and license. Linking or
citing them through OpenQuest does not place them under CC0 or CC BY 4.0.

## Trademark

The software license does not grant rights to use OpenQuest branding in a way
that suggests an unofficial service is the official network. See
[TRADEMARKS.md](./TRADEMARKS.md).
